Why open source?

Business software should help you build independence, not make your company more dependent on the vendor that sold it to you.

  • Inspect how the system works.
  • Contribute improvements and extensions.
  • Keep your workflows portable as the platform evolves.
  • Self-host components where practical instead of being permanently locked in.

Start small.

You do not need to migrate your whole business on day one. Start free, organize one part of the work, and expand when 3DVR earns the next job.
