A few common questions

Do we need to replace our existing tools?

We start by looking at what you already use. A small integration or a clearer checklist may be enough; a new platform should earn its place.

Can you help with an existing self-hosted n8n setup?

Yes. We can review the workflow and hosting arrangement, identify a bounded improvement, and agree on access and scope before making changes.

Do you only work in San Diego?

Thomas is based in San Diego. We also work remotely with owners and teams elsewhere.

What does a project cost?

It depends on the workflow, access, and level of support. Tell us what you want to improve, and we can discuss a useful first step and quote before work starts.

What are you building?

Tell us a little about your business, the tools you use, and one thing you wish worked better. A conversation is a good place to start.
