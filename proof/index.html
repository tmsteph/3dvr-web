Case study #001

Can 3DVR make 3DVR easier to buy?

The first problem is not lack of software. It is friction between interest and becoming a recurring customer.

Shipped: paid plans route to a simple Stripe subscription checkout without requiring a portal account first.
Shipped: the paid-plan buttons now say exactly what happens — pay securely — instead of promising an account-first portal flow.
Shipped: feature-branch Vercel previews are skipped so rapid building does not consume the deployment quota needed for production.
Now measuring: whether simpler checkout and clearer language turn more interested visitors into paying subscribers.

The Builder promise we are testing

Every month, ship one meaningful improvement that helps the business make money, save time, or operate more calmly.

For 3DVR itself, the first measurable outcome is recurring revenue. We will update this page as the numbers move. If they do not move, that is part of the proof too.
