Small animals. Huge personalities.

Cats

Cats are curious, expressive companions known for their independence, playfulness, and surprisingly strong opinions about boxes, windows, and where you are allowed to sit.

🐈

Curious by nature

Cats explore with their eyes, ears, whiskers, paws, and noses. New sounds, smells, shelves, and cardboard boxes can become instant investigations.

Quiet communicators

Tail position, ears, posture, purring, chirps, meows, and slow blinks all help cats communicate how they feel.

Built to play

Short bursts of chasing, pouncing, climbing, scratching, and puzzle-like play help keep indoor cats active and engaged.

Comfort matters

Most cats appreciate clean water, a safe sleeping place, scratching surfaces, a clean litter area, and a little control over their own space.

Every cat is different. Some are social shadows, some are dignified roommates, and some need more time and space before they decide you are acceptable.
